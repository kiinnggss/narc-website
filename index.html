About the Corps

Order, Character, and Community Support

Nigerian Action Rangers Corps is a structured voluntary corps focused on instilling discipline, physical readiness, and civic responsibility among Nigerian youths and adults.

Members train in parade drill, crowd coordination, community safety support, and leadership so every unit can represent its state command with integrity and restraint.

01

Structured State Commands

Recruits are organized by state of residence for clear local coordination, training schedules, and unit accountability.

02

Open, Free Intake

Prospective members register directly online at no cost. State officers review the register and contact shortlisted applicants by phone.

03

Visible Civic Conduct

Every member upholds the corps motto of Discipline during public parades, community outreach, and civic support duties.

What We Do

Core Pillars of the Corps

Discipline & Parade Drill

Regular formation drills, turnout inspections, and chain-of-command training that build punctuality, composure, and teamwork.

Community Safety Support

Orderly crowd assistance, civic event marshaling, and local awareness drives supporting safer, better-organized neighborhoods.

Youth Mentorship & Leadership

Practical mentorship from senior corps officers that prepares young Nigerians to lead teams responsibly and serve their states.
